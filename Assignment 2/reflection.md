# Did chunking boundaries affect retrieval quality for any specific queries?

Chunk boundaries created a possible risk for Query 7: one rebooking chunk ends at the Platinum Elite heading, while the benefits continue in the next chunk. Retrieving only part of that section could produce an incomplete comparison. The current splitter uses an 800-token limit, 150-token overlap, and paragraph/sentence separators, producing 13 chunks across six documents. These settings help preserve context but do not guarantee that entire policy sections stay together. I did not compare alternative chunk sizes or inspect every query's retrieved passages, so I cannot confirm that boundaries caused the observed omissions; retrieval ranking and answer generation could also explain them.
